Privacy and terms

FocusTime is Cooptimize's internal planning app, for Cooptimize accounts only. This page covers the app in the browser and the FocusTime bot in Microsoft Teams. Last updated November 5, 2026.

Privacy · Terms of use · Questions

Privacy

What FocusTime reads

With your Microsoft sign-in, FocusTime reads what it needs to plan your week: your focus blocks and time off from Dataverse, your Outlook calendar, the Azure DevOps items for the clients you work on, the Teams chats registered for those clients, and your own Google calendar if you connect it. For teammates it reads only free or busy times, an "away" for time off, a presence word and their profile photo.

What it keeps

Your plan (picks, drafts, follow-ups, notes you type), your settings, and a log of every change it made outside FocusTime so you can undo it. Your sign-in tokens and DevOps keys are kept encrypted and never leave the server. Teammates' event details, status messages and presence are never kept.

In Teams

The FocusTime bot answers only in your own 1:1 chat with it, and only when you message it. To reply it keeps where that chat is (Teams' chat address), the state of the preview cards it sent you, and a one-way fingerprint of each message's id for a day so it answers once. It doesn't keep what you wrote; your words are handled like a request in the app. Nothing changes until you tap Confirm. It never posts anywhere else, and it doesn't message you first unless you turn that on in Settings › Notifications.

AI

AI features run only when your FocusTime admin has turned them on for the team and for that client. Requests go to the model provider with storage off, one client at a time, never with email addresses, secrets or another client's details. FocusTime keeps the counts and cost of each request, never its text.

Your choices

Settings › Data › Export my data gives you everything kept for you as a file. Delete my data signs you out and removes it: the Teams bot's records at once, everything else 30 days later. Settings › Data › Assistant history › Clear removes what you asked the assistant, in the app and in Teams. Removing the app in Teams stops the bot from reaching you.

Terms of use

FocusTime is for Cooptimize staff, for Cooptimize work. Use it with your own account only. Client data you see in it stays with that client: don't copy it into another client's work. FocusTime suggests; you decide. Every change it makes to DevOps, Outlook, Teams or Azure shows a preview first and happens only when you confirm, and you remain responsible for what you confirm. Cooptimize's policies for acceptable use and client confidentiality apply here as they do everywhere else. The app may change or be unavailable at times; your plan is kept and comes back when it does.

Questions

Ask your FocusTime admin, or use Send feedback in the app's menu.
