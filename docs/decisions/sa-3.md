## SA-3: Settings regrouped: eleven sections, Reminders and notices in one place (Nov 18)

Unnumbered until merge.

| - | Done | **The Teams times change.** Midday check-in and Close out in Teams come at the app's times: the check-in at the afternoon's start plus the check-in reminder's minutes (12:00 when a day has no lunch), the close out at the day's end less the close out reminder's minutes (15 by default; Teams' own default was 30). Teams' own fields (`teams_checkin_at`, `teams_closeout_minutes`) are no longer read for a time or written, and stay stored. A person with Teams messages on whose Teams times differed sees one note on each row, "Teams used 12:00 PM. It now uses this time." with Got it (`settings.note_seen`, the `teams_time_note` mark, once). Teams' close out goes at least 5 minutes before the day ends (Teams' own minimum), so an app Close out of 0 to 4 minutes still reaches Teams. |
