## ST-1: Status: what is on the report and what isn't yet (Nov 19)

Unnumbered until merge. Named `st-1-status.md` because `st-1.md` is the Settings overhaul's (PR #82), which used the same code. The before and after counts and the tables of every kind of line and every control are in `docs/ux/st1/after.md`.

| # | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| ST-1.1 | Done | Every line under a feature is in exactly one of three states, with one name everywhere (the page, Preview, the phone, messages, the Guide): **On the report** (in DevOps now, so Monday's report shows it), **Ready to push** (mine, written or added, not in DevOps yet) and **Suggested** (written for me, by the AI or from what I worked on when AI is off; nothing happens until I add it). One pure rule, `ui/status-states.ts` (`stateOf`, `STATE_NAME`, `STATE_SHAPE`), with STEP 0.3's table as its tests. |
| ST-1.2 | Done | The three differ in shape as well as color: On the report is a plain bullet, Ready to push a solid amber left edge with the words "Ready to push", Suggested a dashed blue box with the word "Suggested". Each says its name to a screen reader (`aria-label`, and `data-state` for tests). A key under the header says the three once, with a few words each, in Write. |
| ST-1.3 | Done | What the AI writes is always Suggested until Add. Suggest a line (once Draft, one feature) and Suggest for all (once Draft all, one AI request per client, as before) put their text in the feature's suggestion box, replacing what was there; before ST-1 they went straight among the lines to push. No AI call was added or changed. |
| ST-1.4 | Done | A suggestion has Add (it becomes Ready to push), Edit (it opens as my line with its text) and Dismiss. What it was based on is one quiet link in it ("Based on 2 things you worked on") that opens what "What a draft uses" showed. GD-2's "Another client's name was removed" is one short line inside that suggestion, no longer a sentence above the client. Once added or dismissed it is gone for this report (SR-3's dismissed list in the browser). |
| ST-1.5 | Done | A feature has two labelled parts. **On Monday's report**, with how many lines or "nothing yet": each line one sentence followed by "· author, day" ("You" on mine and "· Coop" on Coop's in Write; Preview names everyone, as the email does; the full time on hover), Edit and Delete after my own, nothing pushed to the row's far edge. **Not on it yet**, on the sunken ground under a dashed rule, in Write and where I can write: my Ready to push lines, then the suggestion, then Write a line and Suggest a line (Suggest a line only where AI is on for the client). |
| ST-1.6 | Done | An edit or a delete of my own comment waits in Not on it yet as a Ready to push line ("Ready to push · Edit of your line"; "Ready to push · Deletes your line" with Keep it), while the bullet above keeps what DevOps has: the bullet is what Monday's report would say now.
