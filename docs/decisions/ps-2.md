| - | Done | PS-2: why. Tanner (Nov 12, with a screenshot): "there is a bug in the ui. the little bit of pixels under the story names appear to be the user story id". A bug from PS-1. No partly shown line of text anywhere on Day, Week or the block panel: a line shows whole or not at all. |
| - | Done | Two causes, both in PS-1's rule that makes a title link a full target (`:is(main.grid, .day-view, aside.block-panel) :is(a[data-card], .aw-row-title, .pf-title-link)` in `ui/styles.css`), which padded the clipping box and gave it a minimum height. 1: a to-do's title link with its "#id" cue (`.pr-link.pr-cue`, Day's rows beside the Timeline) is one line high with the "#id" wrapping onto a hidden second line when it doesn't fit; the minimum height made the link taller than its line, so the top of the hidden "#id" line showed (3px). 2: a clamp cuts at the padding edge, not the text's, so the bottom padding showed the top of a two-line title's third line: on Week's and Day's cards (5px), the panel's ON THIS BLOCK and Assigned to you rows (10px) and the phone (11px). |
| - | Done | The fix, at the rule: the target's height comes from a transparent top and bottom border (a browser draws borders in whole pixels, so the top is half the room rounded down and the bottom the rest rounded up: the target or less than a pixel more, the text where padding put it) on a content-box, never padding or a minimum height. A clip cuts at the padding edge, inside the border, so the box keeps PS-1's 28 px (44 px on the phone) while what shows ends at the last whole line; `.pr-cue` keeps its one-line height and adds the border around it, so its "#id" shows on the title's line when it fits and not at all when it doesn't, as before. Titles stay at two lines with the full title on hover. No markup changed. |
| - | Done | A title that wraps a title link (`.pr-title`, `.aw-row-title`, `.aw-feat-name` around an `a[data-card]`) leaves the clamp and the target to the link: it adds no border of its own (the row would grow twice) and no longer clips, so the link's focus ring shows whole. Before, the wrapper cut the ring of every linked title on cards, in the panel's rows and on its feature headers (measured; it was so on main too). |
| - | Done | The check (`clippedLines` and `cutFocusRings` in `e2e/ps1-audit.ts`, run by `e2e/ps1.test.ts`): on Week, Week's panel, Day with its panel (folds open), Day's Timeline and the phone's block, in the ordinary and heavy states, at 390, 1280 and 1440 wide, at Default and Larger text: every line of text inside elements that clip (overflow hidden or clip, a line clamp; scroll containers and the page's own frame left out) is fully inside what they show or fully outside, and no title link's focus ring crosses an element that clips.
