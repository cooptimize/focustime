## RS-2: Day's catch-up day, Week's client colors, loading (Nov 13)

Unnumbered until merge.

| # | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| - | Done | RS-2: why. The product owner, on a Friday: "the timeline on the Day view appears to be completely broken", "maybe the day timeline was just loading really slow", "the Week view used to have a color coded block look with the color assigned to each customer... can we bring this back?" and "the left and right arrows on the Week view do not appear to be functional." His status bar showed Outlook failed and Google needing sign-in at the time. Three reports, each proven before a fix (STEP 0), then polish after the RS-1 restyle. |
| - | Done | Day on the catch-up day. Proven: Day's blocks came from `slotsOf` (`plan/todayday.ts`), which kept only halves with a focus block, an Open half-day or an evening; a catch-up day has neither, so Day's Timeline was empty, its header had no Add, Start focus never showed and the phone's Day was empty, while Day's Cards (Week's grid for one day) drew the two Catch-up cards. Now one answer, `dayBlocks` in `plan/dayblocks.ts`, decides what a day holds for Week's grid, Day's Cards, Day's Timeline and the phone: a weekend day is its own quiet column, a PTO day or holiday one hatched cell, a catch-up day with no working hours one dashed Catch-up cell, any other day its halves (plus the evening when it has one). A working catch-up day's halves are blocks named Catch-up in the neutral color; Add, tick, move, timebox and Start focus work there, with Undo. Hiding the catch-up day on Week (`ui.set_week_days`) doesn't hide it on Day. `plan/dayblocks.test.ts` walks every weekday of the builder, sample, neutral and demo weeks with and without a working Friday; `e2e/rs2-day.test.ts` does it in the browser. |
| - | Done | Week's client colors. Proven: the RS-1 restyle left Week's cards on the plain surface with only the edge in the client's color, and a past card went grey (faint, a thin edge). Now a client's card is filled in its color at the timeline bands' strength (`--client-tint` over the surface) with the full 5 px edge, on Week and on Day; a past card keeps the full edge and a lighter fill (60% of the tint), never the plain surface (style guide section 10, replacing "past (faint)"). Catch-up and Open stay neutral. Neutral's dark tint goes from 14% to 13% so faint text on the yellow client fill passes AA (4.47:1 before); every text color passes on every client fill, today and past, in every theme (`ui/rs1-contrast.test.ts`, `e2e/rs2-colors.test.ts`). |
| - | Done | Changing week or day. Proven: the arrows worked, but the screen kept the old week and its header until the server answered, and the server waited for every calendar: an Outlook read that hangs held week.load for its full 20 s timeout even with an earlier copy (20007 ms). The only sign was the status bar's Refresh saying Loading…. Now the header names the asked week or day within 150 ms of the press (Previous, Next, `[` and `]`, a day name, the date picker, This week); the old blocks dim at once (no transition, so reduced motion has nothing to cut) under one "Loading…" with `aria-busy`, and can't be acted on; presses add up from the asked week, so three fast presses end three weeks on; an answer for a week no longer asked never paints. A failed read says "This week didn't load" or "This day didn't load" with Retry; the address keeps what was asked and Back works. The status bar's Refresh stays off during the read but keeps its word, so the screen says Loading… once. |
| - | Done | The server answers within about 3 s from an earlier copy (C5): a calendar read that takes longer than `answerWithinMs` (3000) with a snapshot returns the snapshot, marked unreachable with its time ("Outlook unreachable. Measured (`actions/slow-source.test.ts`, RS2_MEASURE): Outlook failing after 20 s with a copy, 20007 ms before, 3002 ms after; the other three cases unchanged (21, 4 and 20002 ms). |
