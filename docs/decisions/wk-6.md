## WK-6: Meetings named on the day in focus, a call's state on its meeting, no call chips (Nov 18)

Unnumbered until merge.

| # | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| - | Done | What more is kept of a meeting: one column, `meeting_occurrences.event_key`, the occurrence's Graph `iCalUId` (an id, nothing else), read with the series' instances. The calendar read adds `iCalUId` to its `$select`, held in memory on the event (`CalEvent.key`) like its other fields and never stored. Nothing else of a meeting is read, kept or sent. STEP 0.4: a registered call kept only its series master id (per mailbox) or none (registered by link), and its occurrences only times, so a call could be tied to its meeting by time alone. |
| - | Done | The tie (`tieCalls`, `ui/meetings/calls.ts`): by the meeting itself first (the occurrence's key, or a calendar call's iCalUId, against the event's), then by the same start and end only when exactly one meeting fits that isn't another call's keyed meeting. A moved meeting keeps its call; two meetings at the same time never share one guessed call. |
| - | Done | No call chips on any block card, on Week or on Day; no call mark inside a card. A call shows only while it needs me (`needsMe`, the callsNeedingMe rule); a reviewed call is a plain meeting. |
