## PK-4: Your Pocket recordings, found by themselves and counted as yours (Nov 18)

- **Set my Pocket address on a shared key.** The brief accepts an address when it is the recorder on a recording the key just listed. On an organization key that lists a colleague who isn't a FocusTime person, a person could type the colleague's address and have their recordings listed. FocusTime refuses addresses other FocusTime people are known by, refuses in the same words whatever the reason, and allows 5 tries a list, but can't tell a colleague's personal address from mine. Pocket's docs now speak of user keys: a personal key could be told from an organization key by the users list (`GET /public/users`) refusing it ("User API keys are rejected"), and only a personal key's recorders accepted. That needs a fourth call to Pocket, not in this step.
- **What "a recording" is.** Pocket's docs give `state` no values, so it isn't read; an item is a recording when it has a length above 0 and a time. If Pocket lists summaries or uploads in progress with a length, they'd count as recordings.
- **Items under another address aren't asked whether they are recordings once mine are found by address.** That reads less of someone else's items, so such an item counts as "under an address FocusTime doesn't know you by" even when it has no length. The counts are slightly off in that case.
- **Check Pocket again within 5 minutes answers from the held list.** Read as "at most one list in 5 minutes per person" for every way in, including the button. The exception is a changed Pocket address, which asks once more so the new address shows at once.
- **Setting an address only from Calls.** Settings › Connections › Pocket recorder lists my addresses and removes them; adding one is from Calls' left-out line, where the list it is checked against was just read. Should Settings add one too?
- **CLAUDE.md's "overlaps for at least half its length".** The Pocket sentence in the Meeting transcripts rule still describes MC-2b's match. PK-4 also takes a recording with at least half of itself, and 5 minutes, inside the call. Changed in one place only as the brief asked; the product owner may want this phrase updated too.
- **The one-recorder rule with a FocusTime colleague's address.** When the only recorder under the key is another FocusTime person, nothing is kept (MC-2b would have kept it). That shows less.
- **A hidden recording's row and its hide.** A recording's hide isn't counted among "no longer in the list", and is removed only with Put back from its row or at 60 days.
- **Other addresses of a FocusTime person.** "Another FocusTime person's address" means their sign-in email and UPN, for people not deleted. A colleague's alias, a client login or a departed person's address isn't known, so it could be accepted as a Pocket address if it recorded under the key, and an address once accepted isn't checked again if its owner later signs in. (From the diff review.)
- **The 5 tries reset with each new list.** A new list (after 5 minutes, a new key, or a changed address) allows 5 more. Since every refusal reads the same, this only tells a person which outside addresses recorded under their own key. (From the diff review.)
- **A colleague outside the work domain.** Since the walkthrough, an address on the person's work domain other than their own is a colleague's. A colleague's personal address on a shared key still can't be told from the person's own, so it could be set as theirs; Settings asks for a key made for the person alone. Asking Pocket's List organization users would settle it for organization keys, but it is a fourth call (above).
- **A closed call opens again when a recording fits it.** MC-2b's rule (a call closed with no transcript opens when my Pocket recording goes with it) now happens as Calls opens, not on a press. The walkthrough found that surprising. Taken: keep MC-2b's rule; the row says Pocket recording found. A line saying why it opened is a later wording step.
