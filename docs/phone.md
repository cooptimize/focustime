# FocusTime on a phone

Under 600 px wide FocusTime is for checking and doing; planning a week and Ahead are made for a bigger screen (MB-1).

## The bar

At the bottom: **Today**, **Week**, the round **+** (Capture: one line saved at once), **Inbox** (with the Needs you count) and **Ask** (the assistant, over the whole screen). At the top: the FocusTime mark, the **status dot** and **⋯**.

- The dot is green when every source is live, amber when some data is older, red when a source needs attention. Tap it for each source, Retry or Reconnect, the VM, Refresh, and a link to Settings to connect or disconnect.
- Ahead isn't in the bar. A link to Ahead shows "Ahead is made for a bigger screen"; Open anyway shows it read only for the rest of the session.

## Today

FocusTime opens on Today.

- **Now**: the block you're in (until when and how long is left), the meeting under way or the next one, and the next to-do with **Start** and **Done**. Between blocks it says "Free until 1:00"; after the last block, "Day done".
- **Needs you**: up to 3 late or urgent things, then "N more in Inbox".
- **To-dos**, by block: tick the box when it's done, or tap the to-do for Done, Move to tomorrow, Snooze to this afternoon, Add time and Open in DevOps. Changes happen at once; Undo is in the message at the bottom.
- **Next up**: the rest of today's meetings and blocks.
- In the morning a **Plan today** card, near the end of the day **Wrap up the day**.

## Week

One day at a time; tap a day chip or swipe. Each to-do shows its title on its own line and its time and size underneath; ⋯ opens the same sheet as on Today. A block's agenda is folded ("Agenda · 2 meetings · 2h free"); tap to open it. Musts at the top name each client with its count. Plan and Team are read only on a phone.
