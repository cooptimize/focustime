# U5.1: Today and Week fixes after the overhaul

Seen live at 1920x1080, fixed in two commits (D404 to D408). Screenshots are from demo mode, dark, under `screens/u5.1/`.

## What changed

- **Today width.** Full width minus the 24 px gutters at every size, like Week. Blocks share it equally: three blocks
  at 1920 are about 610 px each (were about 370). One block is capped at 900 px and left-aligned.
- **Card overflow.** One rule for every row in a card (meeting, pick, all-day, before and after, left off): one line,
  an ellipsis, the full text on hover and in the detail panel. Cards clip, so nothing paints outside them, on Today,
  Week and Plan the day. Meeting times have one fixed width so titles align. A pick's #id cue drops before its title
  shortens.
- **Status bar.** Borrowed time, the Timelines note and every other notice are chips in the bar's one row, never a
  line under it.
- **Week card headers.** The text column keeps at least 200 px at 1920 and the rail takes the rest (about 150 px on a
  400 px card). The time range drops out of the header before the client name shortens; 12-character names fit.
- **All-day events.** One chip per day the event covers, in the same style every day, including PTO and Catch-up
  days; no more text lines for them inside those cells. Two or more stack.
- **Timelines switch (D407, reverses D383's "always on").** V cycles business hours, 12 hours and 24 hours (H still
  works); Week's ⋯ lists them under Timelines; Settings > Display sets the default. The rail now covers that range,
  with time outside working hours shaded.
- **Personal events switch (D408).** G, Week's ⋯, a new ⋯ on Today's header and Settings > Display, saved as
  `[ui] show_personal`. Hidden events still count in risk and free time and risk reasons still name them; the status
  bar shows "N personal hidden" while it is off, and clicking it shows them again.

## Limits

- At 24 hours the rail is about 17 px per hour at 1920: meetings read as thin bands with their titles on hover.
- A weekend flex session in the morning is still named "Evening" on Today (existing naming, not changed here).
- Plan the day is not affected by the Personal events switch.
