# PH-2: what the phone's Week, another day and the Inbox can reach, before and after

STEP 0.3's list. Each row is something the phone could do or reach on `main` before PH-2 on one of the three screens,
where it was, and where it is after PH-2. Nothing becomes unreachable. What has no place on the new screens is
reached from the to-do's sheet, a block's Add (the block's sheet), a block's name (the same sheet, open on the
block), the Inbox's lists, Settings, or Desktop version, and the row says which. "Another day" is Day for a date
other than today (`#/day/<date>`). Today's Day keeps PH-1's top (no header; `docs/ux/ph1/reach.md`).

Phone only. At 768 and wider nothing here changes.
