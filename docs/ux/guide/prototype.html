Chapter 3

How much fits in a block?

A block is where you work: half a day for one client. Hours are how big the work is. Add stories and watch them fill the free time. This is the same math FocusTime uses on every card.

One focus blockNorthwind · Mon PM · 4h free after meetings
Empty block 0h of 4h
S is 1h. M is 2h. L is 4h. One unit everywhere: hours.
Sizing is optional. An unsized story counts as 2h so the math still works, and nothing waits on it. Size it when you know.
Anything past the free time spills over, shown in amber, never red: it's a nudge to move one, not a mistake.
For the week, a feature is "about 6h". Once its stories are sized, they add up to its size automatically. Whole hours, no decimals, ever.
Tap a story chip to remove it.

Chapter 6

Questions people ask first

Where do my clients and blocks come from?

From Focus Time in Dataverse, the schedule Cooptimize already keeps. If a block is wrong, change it there and FocusTime picks it up on the next refresh.

What does FocusTime change in DevOps or Outlook?

Only what you confirm. Every change shows a preview first, is checked afterwards, and can be undone from Settings > Data. In Outlook it only edits events it created, or your own events with no one else on them. It never deletes anything in DevOps.

Why is "over" amber and not red?

Because plans change and that's normal. Red is only for real trouble, like a block at risk or a follow-up that's overdue.

Do I need to connect my personal calendar?

No. Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, personal events count against your free time so the plan stays realistic, and you can hide them with G.

Can anyone else see my plan?

No. Your plan, notes, follow-ups and settings are yours. Teammates only ever see your free and busy times and your Teams presence, the same as in Outlook and Teams today.

Where do I add my DevOps write access?

Settings > Clients, on each client: enter a personal access token with work item read and write. FocusTime checks it against your DevOps identity, stores it encrypted, and never shows it again.
