# Guide text waiting for UI-5

Words for the Guide when UI-5 rebuilds it. The Guide itself (`ui/guide/`) is not changed by the steps that add text here.

## On a phone (MB-1)

**FocusTime on your phone.** The bar at the bottom has Today, Week, a round + to capture, the Inbox and Ask. The dot at the top shows how your sources are: tap it to refresh or fix one.

**Today first.** Now shows the block you're in, how long is left, your next meeting and your next to-do with Start and Done. Tap any to-do for Done, Move to tomorrow, Snooze to this afternoon, Add time or Open in DevOps; Undo is in the message that follows.

**Week, one day at a time.** Titles get their own line; the agenda is folded until you tap it.

**Ahead is for a bigger screen.** On a phone it asks first; Open anyway shows it read only.

Do it now links: Capture (`#/today` then the + in the bar), Sources (the status dot), Week (`#/week`).
